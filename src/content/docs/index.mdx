## How the reference is checked

- Facts come from the official docs ({facts.docsPages} pages), the changelog, `--help` output, and headless runs of {facts.targetVersion}.
- Every section names its sources. Features that are not generally available carry a status label: Beta, Preview, Experimental, Undocumented, Deprecated, or Removed.
- Where the docs, the changelog, and the binary disagree, the page shows both readings.
- An independent reviewer checked every chapter against the sources before publication.
